# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We pledge to make participation in Create: Cobblemon Kinetics a welcoming,
safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and
orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, healthy,
collaborative community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- showing empathy, patience, and respect for different viewpoints and levels
  of modding experience;
- giving technical feedback about the change rather than the person;
- accepting constructive feedback and correcting mistakes;
- keeping issue reports reproducible and review discussions on topic;
- crediting contributors and respecting licenses, privacy, and attribution;
- using inclusive language and helping newcomers find relevant documentation;
  and
- prioritizing the health of the project and community over individual ego.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or discriminatory comments;
- sexualized language, imagery, or attention;
- trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- pressuring maintainers or contributors through repeated demands, entitlement,
  or off-platform contact;
- impersonation, plagiarism, license violations, or knowingly false reports;
- evading moderation or encouraging others to violate this policy; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

Disagreement with a design decision, a closed issue, or a rejected pull
request does not justify abusive behavior.

## Scope

This policy applies in all official project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, code review, release channels, community chat, and
project events. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project,
such as through an official account or at an event.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Use an
available private repository contact or moderation channel. If no private
channel is visible, open a brief public issue requesting private maintainer
contact **without** describing the incident or identifying the people involved.

Reports should include relevant links, dates, context, and any immediate safety
concern. Do not retaliate against someone for making a good-faith report.
Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible,
but cannot promise absolute confidentiality when safety or legal obligations
require action.

If a report concerns a maintainer, that person must not participate in handling
the report. The remaining maintainers should seek an impartial reviewer when
needed.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this conduct
reporting path.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. Responses will be
proportionate to context, severity, impact, and prior behavior and may include:

1. **Correction:** a private or public explanation and request to stop or edit
   the behavior.
2. **Warning:** a formal warning with conditions for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction:** a time-limited ban from interaction or
   representation.
4. **Permanent restriction:** removal from project spaces for sustained,
   severe, or dangerous behavior.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this policy in good faith may be
removed from project responsibilities by the other maintainers.
